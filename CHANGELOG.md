# Changelog

Every release adds a section headed by its version: the game shows the one it
runs (and the one an update brings) on the title screen, and the release on
GitHub uses it as its notes. Keep each line short: they are read in a small box.

## 2.5.2

- Dropped eggs last 14 s and rise slowly: one that reaches the top is lost
- Wide Wings reaches twice as far; new card Patient Nest: eggs last longer
- New upgrade Long Shells (Fortune): eggs last longer

## 2.5.1

- A tutorial: a tip the first time each thing happens (X goes on, B skips)
- SETTINGS > TUTORIAL turns it off, or on again to see every tip once more

## 2.5.0

- Waves add up: every level has its waves, on a timer, dead or not
- The boss of a world waits until every wave of its level is dead
- From world 3 bats and obstacles get more HP and armor, world by world
- Every boss is tougher than the one before; the last one is the hardest

## 2.4.11

- Every music loop plays equally loud: no dips when the flock or the bats change

## 2.4.10

- Fixed: the music of a new game could start too quiet

## 2.4.9

- CONTROLS > POWER BUTTON: fire powers with X (DOWN on the keyboard)
- The leaderboards update within a minute or two of a game

## 2.4.8

- Clockwork birds are machines: no poison, bleeding, frost or plague
- Y on a gamepad no longer swaps the grade bars for the XP list
- The game cannot open twice (a second launch closes at once)

## 2.4.7

- A lost game can no longer be continued (no CONTINUE after game over)
